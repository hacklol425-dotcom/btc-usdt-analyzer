How the Strategy Works

SMA 20 is the average closing price of the latest 20 candles.

SMA 50 is the average closing price of the latest 50 candles.

BUY: SMA 20 crosses from below SMA 50 to above SMA 50.

SELL: SMA 20 crosses from above SMA 50 to below SMA 50.

The backtest starts with a simulated balance of $1,000. It does not use real money and does not place real trades.
