How the Strategy Works

BUY / simulated entry: SMA 20 crosses from at or below SMA 50 to above SMA 50.

SELL / simulated exit: SMA 20 crosses from at or above SMA 50 to below SMA 50.

A signal is confirmed using a completed five-minute candle. The backtest executes at the next candle's opening price when that next candle is available.

The simulation starts with 1,000 USDT. It uses the available historical Spot candles and does not use real money.
